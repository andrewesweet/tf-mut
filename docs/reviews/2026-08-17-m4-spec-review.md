Adversarial review of the M4 milestone spec (issue #58, first body), posted as a comment on
that issue. **Verdict as delivered: "not ready for an implementation agent"** — six critical
contract failures, six major gaps, previous milestones treated as settled constraints.

All twelve findings accepted, five with chosen-option modifications. Issue #58's body after
this review (revision 2) and the design correction in the same change are the disposition of
record.

## Pattern note

Round six, and the two recurring shapes both reappeared inside a single spec: a mapping
between two address spaces without a fail-closed adapter (C2 — the M3 review's C3 shape, now
in the suggestion generator), and a floor that covered one gate while the side door opened
the other (C1 — the R2-10 shape, one level up). The additions to the checklist question are
mechanical now: *every* new artefact the tool emits into a foreign grammar (an assertion
expression, a rendered value, a written file) needs its own fail-closed adapter and its own
distinct skipped-status — and a safety floor is not a floor until it holds for **every** gate
the unread content could have informed.
